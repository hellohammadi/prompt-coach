Every prompt is checked against seven things a good prompt usually has:

  1. Goal: what exactly the AI should do
  2. Context: background it can't guess
  3. Audience: who the output is for
  4. Output format: shape and length
  5. Constraints: tone, limits, things to avoid
  6. Examples: what good looks like
  7. Success criteria: how you'll judge it
Runs on Gemma 4, Google's open-weight model. Prompts are sent to the model only to check and rewrite them, and nothing is stored.
