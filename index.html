    Prompt Coach as a Chrome extension

    Copying prompts back and forth is a step too many. The extension brings the coach to the box where you already type.

    1. A button beside your prompt boxOn ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, a small Coach button appears next to the text box.
    2. The coach opens in a side panelThe same score, checklist and quick questions, without leaving the page.
    3. One click to use the better promptAccept the rewrite and it replaces your draft, ready to send.

    Also planned: a private mode that runs Gemma on your own computer so prompts never leave your machine, and a report of the gaps you leave out most often.
